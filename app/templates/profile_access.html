You have final authority over who can view or manage your healthcare profile.

💡 Why Role Passcodes Instead of Passwords?

CareNest removes the barrier of complex usernames and passwords. Everyone in your care circle shares one Care Profile ID ({{ profile.care_id }}), but signs in with a role-specific passcode:

  • Elder (You): Sovereign decision-maker. You can edit all medications, appointments, and revoke or regenerate access at any time.
  • Caretaker: Day-to-day healthcare assistant. Can add and edit medications, log doses as taken, and coordinate appointments.
  • Family: Monitoring and peace of mind. View-only access to adherence scores and receives automated email alerts for missed doses.

🔒 Security Note: All passcodes are salted and hashed with bcrypt. They can be rotated instantly below if someone leaves your care team.
